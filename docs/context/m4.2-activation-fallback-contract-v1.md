# M4.2 Activation and Fallback Contract v1

## 1. Status and scope

This document is planning authority input for M4.2 Blocker 7 only. It is not implementation authority and authorizes no production-code, test, telemetry-schema, lifecycle, gate, provider-routing, compiler, reducer, cache, or persistence change.

M4.2 implementation remains blocked. Blocker 8 remains explicitly unresolved. This contract does not reconstruct or modify the decisions for Blockers 1-6 and does not resolve Blockers 8-10.

The controlling evidence is limited to the authorized sections of:

- `context-activation-contract-v1.md` Sections 4, 5, 8, 9, 12, and 14;
- `m4.2-baseline-delta-contract-v1.md` Sections 7, 8, 9, and 11;
- `m4.2-provider-boundary-proof-v1.md` Sections 2, 3, 7, and 10;
- `context-optimization-plan-v1.md` Sections 16.9, 16.10, and 16.13 Blocker 7; and
- the narrow current coordination boundary in `ContextDispatchCoordinator.execute()` and its baseline validation/dispatch helpers.

## 2. Public activation model

The public `CompilationMode` vocabulary remains exactly:

- `BASELINE`
- `OPTIMIZED`

M4.2 lives only under the existing explicit `OPTIMIZED` mode unless separate future authority changes the public activation contract. This contract introduces no `M4_2`, `DELTA`, `REUSE`, `AUTO`, or `OPTIMIZED_EVALUATION` mode. Token-budget, stage-result, heuristic, provider-capability, and other automatic activation are forbidden.

Internal propagation of a resolved typed mode is not another activation source. Invalid internal values remain programmer misuse, and conflicting activation sources must be rejected rather than resolved by implicit precedence.

## 3. Default compatibility

`DEFAULT_MODE = BASELINE`.

An unset activation value and explicit `BASELINE` are behaviorally equivalent. BASELINE preserves the existing path and behavior without migration, persistent state, baseline cache, delta state, provider capability, cleanup, or repair. Disabling optimization requires no state repair.

BASELINE does not change current provider rendering, call cardinality, lifecycle or gate behavior, run-record meaning, custom-executor compatibility, Stage08 behavior, or CLI syntax.

## 4. Meaning of OPTIMIZED

`OPTIMIZED` grants permission to attempt an authorized optimized preparation when the affected stage and executor path are eligible. It does not guarantee selection, reuse, delta reconstruction, optimized dispatch, provider savings, or any other savings.

An execution activated as `OPTIMIZED` may legitimately dispatch BASELINE exactly once when optimization is unavailable, inapplicable, invalid, or fails before dispatch. Activation and dispatch selection are separate decisions.

## 5. M4.2 eligibility gate

All of the following minimum conditions must hold before an M4.2 reconstructed candidate may proceed toward optimized dispatch:

1. The resolved mode is exactly `OPTIMIZED`.
2. The affected stage and executor path are optimization-eligible under existing approved authority.
3. A current fresh exact assembly exists.
4. The candidate baseline is valid under the Blocker 5 contract, including its coordinates, scope, provenance, trust, authority, and metadata requirements.
5. The exact delta and complete reconstruction validate as one whole candidate.
6. The reconstructed effective ordered provider input equals the fresh exact assembly effective ordered provider input.
7. Authority coverage equals 100%.
8. Authority byte mismatch equals 0.
9. Complete component identity, occurrence, duplicate, and order validation passes, with no missing, extra, substituted, transformed, or ambiguous occurrence.
10. No authority conflict affects the implementation.
11. Provider-boundary assumptions make no unsupported reuse or savings claim.

These are cumulative minimum conditions, not independent dispatch authorizations. This contract does not select or authorize any stage-specific implementation beyond existing approved authority.

## 6. Failure classes and fallback boundary

### A. Optimization-specific pre-dispatch failure

An optimization-specific failure while determining applicability, validating the whole reconstructed candidate, or preparing that candidate before any provider or subprocess call causes the optimized candidate to be discarded in full. BASELINE may then be prepared and dispatched exactly once. The optimized provider/subprocess call count is zero. Optimization alone must not introduce a lifecycle failure.

`OPTIMIZATION_NOT_APPLICABLE` preserves its existing meaning for an unavailable or inapplicable optimized path. `OPTIMIZATION_FALLBACK` preserves its existing meaning for an optimization-specific pre-dispatch failure. `OPTIMIZED_READY` means the fully validated optimized preparation is ready for its single dispatch. These coordination outcomes require no telemetry-schema change.

### B. Shared prerequisite failure

A failure of baseline preparation or any other shared/base prerequisite required by both BASELINE and OPTIMIZED is a genuine existing failure. It propagates under existing semantics. It must not be relabeled as an optimization-specific failure, and optimization fallback must not hide it.

### C. Provider failure after dispatch

A provider or subprocess failure after dispatch begins remains the existing failure. There is no BASELINE retry.

### D. Result or response parse failure after dispatch

A result or response parsing failure after dispatch begins remains the existing failure. There is no BASELINE retry.

This classification specifies behavior, not try/catch mechanics.

## 7. Atomic dispatch and call cardinality

No partial optimized reconstruction may be dispatched. The optimized candidate must be completely assembled and must pass every applicable identity, order, content, baseline, delta, reconstruction, metadata, and authority validation before the provider/subprocess call begins.

`MAX_PROVIDER_CALLS_PER_LOGICAL_EXECUTION = 1`.

Optimization must not increase provider-call or subprocess-call cardinality:

- On optimization-specific pre-dispatch failure, the optimized call count is zero and BASELINE may be dispatched once.
- On optimized dispatch, exactly one optimized call is made.
- Once optimized dispatch begins, fallback must not create a second provider or subprocess call for the same logical execution.
- Provider and post-dispatch parse failures do not authorize a BASELINE retry.

## 8. Provider-boundary consequence

The Blocker 6 result is preserved exactly:

`PROVIDER_BOUNDARY_REUSE = NOT_SUPPORTED_BY_CURRENT_BOUNDARY`.

M4.2 activation and fallback therefore have no dependency on provider-cached context, conversation continuation, a reusable provider handle, or provider-side delta support. `OPTIMIZED` activation creates no provider savings claim.

## 9. Lifecycle and authority compatibility

This contract introduces no new lifecycle state, gate result, provider retry state, persisted activation state, or authority-precedence rule. Existing authority conflicts continue to block the affected implementation. Optimization failure alone does not mutate lifecycle authority.

The coordination outcomes in Section 6 are ephemeral descriptions of the existing coordination meanings and do not authorize a telemetry schema change or a new persisted status vocabulary.

## 10. Non-authorization and no-savings boundary

This contract does not authorize M4.2 implementation, provider-boundary reuse, provider/model routing changes, provider retries, persistence, telemetry changes, or changes to production code or tests. It makes no provider, token, byte, cache, percentage, or other optimization savings claim.

Blocker 8 is explicitly unresolved. Evaluation and measurement authority remain outside this decision.
