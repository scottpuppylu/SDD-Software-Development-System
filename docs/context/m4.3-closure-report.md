# M4.3 Closure Report

## 1. Scope

This report performs closure evaluation only for the frozen M4.3 boundary. It does not authorize or implement production changes, test changes, provenance, telemetry changes, persistence, provider reuse, lifecycle changes, public API changes, task identity, token-budget truncation, semantic retrieval or ranking, authority summarization or substitution, implementation slices, or a commit.

The evaluation resolves whether the conservative review of every inventoried operational component is complete. A component need not produce a new reduction policy for M4.3 to close. Any future reduction requires a new, separately approved planning amendment.

## 4. Candidate-resolution summary

The final classifications are internally consistent with the planning inventory and all later evidence rounds. `GIT_HISTORY` is the sole currently reducible component and continues to use only `sdd.git-history.newest8-oldest2-v1`. `SOURCE_CODE`, `TEST_EVIDENCE`, and `STAGE10_CONTEXT_POLICY` remain never reducible within the frozen boundary. The other four components reached `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE / STOP` after their proposed transformations failed the semantic-safety or usefulness gates.

No component was promoted without evidence. There are no new M4.3 candidates and no new M4.3 policies. The absence of a new policy is a legitimate conservative result, not a closure failure.

## 5. Provenance decision

Provenance semantics are conceptually required before any future caller-accessible `UNKNOWN` runtime component could safely become reducible. Trust would have to be established by explicit structural producer provenance and all missing, malformed, lost, ambiguous, caller-supplied, or otherwise unproven provenance would remain exact.

Provenance implementation is not required now. Even under the planning assumption that trusted internal provenance could be proved, none of the evaluated new components passed the independent semantic-safety and usefulness gate. Implementing provenance solely for these rejected candidates would provide no authorized reduction and is therefore unnecessary. This report does not authorize provenance implementation.

## 6. Safety and invariant closure

Closure preserves authority coverage at 100% and authority byte mismatch at 0. `UNKNOWN` and `MIXED_AUTHORITY` remain fail-closed exact, governance remains exact, and no task identity is introduced or inferred. `BASELINE` remains the default and `OPTIMIZED` remains an explicit attempt only.

There is no new lifecycle or provider-call behavior: a candidate must still be fully prepared before dispatch, partial optimized dispatch and post-dispatch retry remain forbidden, and the maximum provider or subprocess call count remains one. The provider boundary remains `SELF_CONTAINED_FULL_INPUT`. Provider-side reuse, cross-run trust, and persistence remain unauthorized. There is no public API change, telemetry change, automatic token-budget truncation, semantic retrieval or ranking, or authority summarization or substitution.

## 7. Measurement / provider-usage claims

M4.3 makes no provider-token savings claim and no byte or percentage reduction claim for new candidates. Codex CLI `tokens used` is operational evidence only and is not provider-usage evidence. Unavailable provider usage must remain unavailable and must not be recorded or interpreted as zero. Telemetry schema remains `1.0.4` with no change.

## 8. Implementation disposition

No production implementation remains warranted by M4.3. No new candidate survived the conservative semantic-safety and usefulness evaluation, and the existing `GIT_HISTORY` behavior remains unchanged. The implementation disposition is `NO_FURTHER_IMPLEMENTATION`.

The component-specific evidence gaps recorded for stopped candidates are conditions for any future separately approved proposal; they are not unresolved blockers to conservative M4.3 closure.

## 9. Final M4.3 decision

`M4_3_RESULT = PASS`.

The inventory is resolved, classifications are internally consistent, no authority conflict or unauthorized promotion remains, no unsupported policy is introduced, no unsafe implementation is required, and every frozen invariant is preserved. M4.3 is therefore `COMPLETE` and `CLOSED`, with no unresolved closure blockers.
